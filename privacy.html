Privacy notice

Privacy on this site

Last updated 23 September 2026. This notice covers the website agenticcad.github.io/agenticcad. The AgenticCAD application itself runs on your own computer and sends nothing to us.

What this site collects

Nothing, unless you accept analytics cookies. There are no accounts, no forms and no newsletter. If you click Accept on the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used:

  • Pages viewed, time on page, the page you came from, and clicks on the download buttons (with the release version).
  • Approximate location (country or city) derived by Google from your IP address, and your browser, device type and screen size.
  • A random identifier stored in cookies (_ga, _ga_*, kept for up to 2 years) so return visits can be counted as one visitor.

Google Analytics does not log or store your IP address: it is used during collection to work out a coarse location and is then discarded. Data is processed by Google under its privacy policy; Google Signals and advertising features are switched off. We keep reports for 14 months.

If you click Decline, or make no choice, no Google script is loaded and no analytics cookie is set. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (agenticcad-consent) so we do not ask again for 12 months. Change it any time with Cookie settings in the footer; declining after accepting removes the analytics cookies for this site.

Third parties that see your request

Like any web page, loading this site sends your IP address and browser details to the servers that serve its parts:

  • GitHub hosts the site (GitHub Pages) and answers the "latest release" lookup that fills the download buttons. GitHub privacy statement.
  • jsDelivr serves the three.js library used by the 3D views, and Google Fonts serves the typefaces.

None of these receive anything from us beyond the request itself, and we do not receive personal data from them.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, remarketing or cross-site tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone other than the processors above.
  • No fingerprinting or tracking when you decline.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the Australian Privacy Act and similar laws you can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be deleted, or object to processing. Because analytics data is pseudonymous we usually cannot link it to you, but we will do what we can. Withdrawing consent is immediate via Cookie settings. Contact: agenticcad@prodevelop.com.au.

The application

The AgenticCAD program you download runs locally. Its only network traffic is the conversation with Anthropic's API (or your Claude Code login) that you configure yourself, plus anything you choose to connect through MCP servers. It has no telemetry.
