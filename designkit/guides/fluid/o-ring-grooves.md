## Rules

| Quantity | Static | Dynamic |
|---|---|---|
| Squeeze (of the cross-section) | 15 to 30 % (face seals 20 to 30 %) | 10 to 20 % |
| Groove fill | ≤ 85 % | ≤ 85 % |
| Stretch on the inner diameter | ≤ 5 % | ≤ 3 % |

- Groove depth ≈ CS × (1 − squeeze): 0.75 × CS static face, 0.8 to 0.85 × CS radial.
- Groove width ≈ 1.3 to 1.5 × CS so the ring has room to deform.
- Face seals: for internal pressure put the ring against the groove's outer wall (groove OD ≈ ring OD); for vacuum
  or external pressure, against the inner wall.
- Chamfer entry edges 15° to 20° so the ring isn't cut on assembly; groove surfaces smooth (Ra 1.6 or better static).

## Common cross-sections (mm)

1.0, 1.5, 1.78, 2.0, 2.5, 2.62, 3.0, 3.53, 4.0, 5.33 (1.78, 2.62, 3.53 and 5.33 are the AS568 series).

## Printed parts

Printed groove walls leak along layer lines under pressure: seal against a machined or smooth face, use a face seal
with 25 to 30 % squeeze, or a gasket in TPU.
