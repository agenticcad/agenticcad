---
- Walls ≥ 1 mm (0.6 mm supported), pins ≥ 0.8 mm; fine detail down to ~0.1 mm.
- Orient to minimise the cross-section each layer peels from the film; 30° to 45° tilts are common. Supports go on
  downward-facing surfaces and leave marks: keep them off cosmetic faces.
- Hollow large parts (2 to 3 mm shell) with at least two drain holes ≥ 3.5 mm so resin drains and suction breaks.
- Clearances: 0.05 to 0.1 mm per side for sliding fits; holes print slightly small.
- Parts shrink and warp a little on post-cure; standard resins are brittle, use tough/ABS-like resins for snap fits.
