---
title: Designing for injection moulding
category: manufacturing
tags: [injection moulding, draft, wall thickness, rib, boss, undercut, gate]
summary: Uniform walls, draft angles, ribs and bosses, radii and undercuts for injection-moulded plastic parts
related: [enclosures/enclosure-design, tolerances/materials, screw_boss]
---
- Uniform walls: 1 to 3 mm for most parts (ABS 1.2 to 3.5, PP 0.8 to 3, PC 1 to 4). Thick sections sink and void;
  core them out.
- Draft on every wall that slides out of the tool: ≥ 1° (0.5° minimum), 3° or more on textured faces.
- Ribs 50 to 60 % of the wall thickness, ≤ 3 × wall tall, spaced ≥ 2 × wall apart.
- Bosses: outer Ø ≈ 2 × the screw, wall ≈ 60 % of nominal, tied to walls with ribs; 0.5° draft on the core pin.
- Inside radii ≥ 0.5 × wall, outside radius = inside + wall: sharp corners concentrate stress and slow the flow.
- Undercuts need side actions or lifters (cost); design snap fits and holes in the pull direction where possible.
- Leave a flat, hidden area for the gate and ejector pins.
