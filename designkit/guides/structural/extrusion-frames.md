## Profiles

| Series | Slot | Screws | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 (2020, 2040) | 6 mm | M5 | 3D printers, small machines |
| 30 (3030) | 8 mm | M6 | Benches, CNC frames |
| 40 (4040, 4080) | 8 or 10 mm | M8 | Machine frames, heavy fixtures |

2040 is roughly 4 times stiffer than 2020 in its tall direction: use it for spans that carry load.

## Joining

- Corner brackets: quick and adjustable. One per inside corner, two for stiffness.
- End-tapped joints: tap the centre bore (M5 in 2020) and screw through a hole in the crossing profile.
  Very rigid and clean; needs an access hole for the key.
- Printed or plate gussets on the outside faces add a lot of racking stiffness.

## T-nuts

- Drop-in (hammer) nuts go in after assembly; slide-in nuts must be loaded before closing the frame.
- Screw length: bracket thickness + about 6 mm into a 20-series nut. Too long bottoms out in the slot.

## Squareness and rigidity

- Cut profiles square (±0.1 mm on length for a square frame).
- Diagonal braces or panels in slots stop racking; a closed box is far stiffer than an open frame.
